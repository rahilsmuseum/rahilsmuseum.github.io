Rahil’s Museum of Digital Artifacts is a repository for archiving and exhibiting Rahil Static’s design work, explorations, ideation processes, research, stories and perspective.

It is an experimental playground where all kinds of ideas are thrown around in an effort to find the best ones. The website itself will see a lot of changes from page to page, old concepts will evaporate and new ones will emerge, but they will all be documented to show progress.

Reason For Existence

When I started designing back in 2020, I was guided by passion, love and appreciation for the medium. Working professionally over the years, I lost touch with what made it so special. I am grateful that I get to work in this industry, but the average 9 to 5 design job doesn’t always allow for unrestricted creative expression. As a result, this museum is a safe space to exhibit all my creative experimentation and storytelling, it serves as a reminder as to why I fell in love with this in the first place.
