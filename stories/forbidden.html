FINAL NOTES

Thank you so much for reading this story. To me this is the best original design work I have done so far... my magnum opus. The idea had been lingering for quite some time. Avant-garde design meets avant-garde storytelling and structure. Modern design to me lacks a certain weight, a certain emotion. My hope with this project was to see if I could evoke that feeling again, something that makes you pause, think and reflect... something worth the meditation.

- Rahil H. Static
